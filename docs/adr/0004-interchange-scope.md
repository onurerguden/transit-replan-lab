# ADR 0004 · Model two documented interchanges

Date 2026-11-11 · Accepted

The municipality identifies Konak and Fahrettin Altay as Metro–Tram connections. Use those two interchange pairs. The timetable sources do not supply the walking time or a wheelchair guarantee for the modeled connectors. Record duration as a simulation assumption and distinguish unknown accessibility from confirmed accessibility.

Source [Municipality transit guide](https://izmir.bel.tr/tr/UlasimHizmetleri/18/81).
